**2. High — Missing-key map operations can loop forever after deletion churn. Reproduced.**

An empty map has capacity 8. Insert and delete keys 0 through 7, one at a time. The count returns to zero, but each bucket becomes a tombstone. `m ^? 100` never returns; the reproduction (`map_tombstones/main.dva`, archived with the report) prints `0` and times out after eight seconds.

Membership ends only at an empty bucket or a matching live entry; the probe wraps without a bound at [runtime.dva:2389](https://github.com/edva-lang/edva/blob/bc20a35ba2dd351bb0c891797a33d1ec8ae74940/src/codegen/runtime.dva#L2389). Deletion and typed lookup contain the same unbounded pattern ([expr.dva:7177](https://github.com/edva-lang/edva/blob/bc20a35ba2dd351bb0c891797a33d1ec8ae74940/src/codegen/expr.dva#L7177)). Growth uses live count, so tombstones can fill the table without triggering growth ([runtime.dva:2238](https://github.com/edva-lang/edva/blob/bc20a35ba2dd351bb0c891797a33d1ec8ae74940/src/codegen/runtime.dva#L2238)). Bound probes by capacity and account for tombstone occupancy when deciding to rebuild the table.

```dva
#use io

m = M()
m ^ 0 = 0

m ^ 3 = 3
deleted3 = m ^- 3

deleted5 = m ^- 5
m ^ 6 = 6
deleted6 = m ^- 6

io::out $ ?m
```

**Acceptance criteria**

- [ ] Missing lookup, membership, and deletion terminate after at most capacity probes.
- [ ] Track tombstone occupancy or rebuild sufficiently early to keep probing bounded.
- [ ] Exercise every bucket becoming a tombstone, then verify missing operations and reinsertion.

Run each reproduction separately with the audited compiler using `timeout 15s ./edva main.dva -r` and a 4 GiB virtual-memory limit. A timeout is expected for the current tombstone-hang reproduction.
