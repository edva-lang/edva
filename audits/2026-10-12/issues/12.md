**12. Design contract — `!<` and `!>` are not logical complements for NaN. Reproduced.**

With `x = 0.0 / 0.0`, the probe (`float_nan_logic/main.dva`, archived with the report) produces false for `x !< 1.0` and true for `!(x < 1.0)`. Ordered >=/<= predicates implement these operators ([match.dva:1498](https://github.com/edva-lang/edva/blob/bc20a35ba2dd351bb0c891797a33d1ec8ae74940/src/codegen/match.dva#L1498)). That is a defensible numeric choice, but conflicts with teaching `!<` as “not less than” ([llms-full.txt:13](https://github.com/edva-lang/edva/blob/bc20a35ba2dd351bb0c891797a33d1ec8ae74940/doc/llms-full.txt#L13)). Specify NaN behavior explicitly, or implement actual complement semantics. Do not silently substitute the two expressions in documentation or compiler rewrites.
